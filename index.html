What changed overall

  • Every slide is in the new Positive Partnerships design, with much less text on screen. The detail now sits in the speaker notes, Facilitator Guide and Participant Workbook.
  • Each session now opens with a session overview slide, and Sessions 2 to 6 also get a title slide. These are the 11 new slides.
  • The clock times are gone from "Today's workshop", and the separate "Today's journey" slide is gone.
  • Several activities now open with a question instead of instructions, such as "A new Year 7 student starts next Monday" and "Imagine a call from the parent of an Autistic student".
  • Long slides were cut back to their main point. The vision slide is now just the question "What does thriving look like for Autistic students in your school?"

    How this was put together

    October slides are the trial deck from the October 2026 trial set. November slides are the 16 November 2026 edit of the new design deck. Both were exported from PowerPoint.

    Slides were matched by their wording, so a November slide sits beside the October slide it came from even when its title changed. The change notes compare what is on the slide. Speaker notes, the Facilitator Guide and the Participant Workbook are not compared here.

    Word counts are approximate and count only the words on the slide. Select any slide to see both versions larger.
